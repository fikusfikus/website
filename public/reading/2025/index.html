My Year of Rest and Relaxation

Ottessa Moshfegh

2018

2025-12-04

Some funny moments, engaging enough. This served as a pretty good way to get me back into reading, super quick and easy. I could see people hating the main character for being such a wastoid but it was clearly exaggerated for comedic effect and it did work on me. If I wanted to pull something from this, I kind of like interpreting the whole book as laying out the exact context and life experienced required to understand one person’s interpretation of a single piece of art. The whole “diving into the unknown” and “she was awake” stuff, this is what she pulled from the 9/11 piece, and we’re given all the pieces to grasping that fully by reading what the book gives us about her. It is sometimes impossible to try and explain why I like a piece of art to somebody without talking about myself way too much, and this book did kind of accomplish that, which I like.
